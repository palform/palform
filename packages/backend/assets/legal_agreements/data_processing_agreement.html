ANNEX II: DESCRIPTION OF THE PROCESSING

Categories of data subjects whose personal data is processed

End-users submitting responses to forms created by the controller via the processor's web-based platform (Palform).

Categories of personal data processed

  • Any personal data requested on the form as created by the controller.
  • Technical data collected directly by the processor, such as the IP address and user agent of the user's browser.

Sensitive data processed and relevant safeguards

The processor itself does not directly process sensitive data about end-users. The controller may decide to add questions to forms that request sensitive data. In doing so, they take full liability for the processing of the sensitive data.

Form responses are end-to-end encrypted using industry-standard cryptography protocols. This means only the end-user submitting the form and the controller have access to plaintext submission data. The processor only has access to the ciphertext version of the data and is therefore physically unable to access any sensitive data that may be contained within it.

Nature of the processing

The processor operates a website through which forms created by the controller can be filled in by end-users. Form responses are stored on the processor's database in an encrypted ciphertext format, and are made available to the controller via the online dashboard. The controller's web browser decrypts the responses, so the plaintext response data is never available to the processor. The cryptographic private keys are held only by the controller, except for a backup stored on the processor's server, encrypted with a passphrase known only to the controller.

Technical data collected about the end-user is used to correctly route and store the response data, and to enable the investigation of technical errors. IP addresses and other identifiable information about the end-user are never retained for longer than is needed to complete the form submission request.

Purpose(s) for which the personal data is processed on behalf of the controller

  • The encrypted form responses are collected as a legitimate interest of the end-user. The end-user reasonably expects and wants their response to be delivered to the controller based on their voluntary completion of the form.
  • Technical data are collected as a legitimate interest of the end-user. The end-user expects their form response to be correctly and securely submitted, stored, and delivered to the controller, and for any errors or bugs to be investigated and resolved promptly.

Duration of the processing

  • Encrypted form responses are stored on the processor's database indefinitely. As such, it is the controller's responsibility to define the duration of the processing. The processor provides a feature to automatically delete response data a certain number of days after their original submission, which the controller can use at its discretion. The controller can also manually delete responses through the processor's online dashboard. When using either the manual or the automated method, the selected responses are immediately and fully deleted from the processor's main database, but may be retained in a backup database for up to 14 days after the date of deletion.
  • Technical data collected about the end-user is normally deleted immediately after the servicing of the end-user's submission request, and is therefore kept for a duration of up to about 1 second. However, in exceptional circumstances, such as errors or bug reports, the data may be retained by the processor for up to 14 days.

ANNEX III TECHNICAL AND ORGANISATIONAL MEASURES INCLUDING TECHNICAL AND ORGANISATIONAL MEASURES TO ENSURE THE SECURITY OF THE DATA

The processor's technical and organisational security measures

  • As mentioned above, all form responses are encrypted end-to-end with keys that are held only be the controller. The processor is physically unable to view the responses itself. If the processor's databases or servers were compromised or a data leak occurred, only the ciphertext data would be affected. Without the decryption keys stored locally on the devices of the administrative users of the controller, this data would be essentially meaningless and random.
  • Form responses are not associated with any IP addresses or other information that could identify the end-user, and as such they are anonymous. The responses are stored with a limited amount of plaintext metadata, specifically the time of submission and the access token used by the end-user to submit the response. These access tokens are generic: one is created each time the controller generates a URL for distributing the form. As such, they cannot be used to identify users individually.
  • The processor's database, including the encrypted form responses but also non-encrypted data such as the structure of the forms and the details of the controller's administrative accounts, is stored encrypted at-rest on the processor's infrastructure.
  • The processor's infrastructure is deployed on the infrastructure of Hetzner Online GmbH. This sub-processor has very high historic rates of uptime and availability. In addition, the processor uses a high-availability Kubernetes cluster for the operation of all of its services, ensuring at least 3 virtual machines are running at any time. In the event of a failure of 1 of them, the remaining 2 will continue functioning without affecting the availability of the service.
  • The processor uses Write-Ahead Logs (WALs) for automated backups of its PostgreSQL database, and retains them for up to 14 days. This ensures the database can be restored to any exact moment in time should an incident occur. The backups are stored on a different cloud provider than the live database to ensure a degree of separation.
  • All of the processor's web infrastructure is made available exclusively via HTTPS, ensuring that no plaintext traffic is sent across the internet.
  • The processor uses a granular and hierarchical authorization model, allowing controllers using its platform to configure their user accounts with specific permissions. This ensures that the minimum possible necessary level of access is granted to each account.
  • The processor uses secure password hashing according to the latest industry standards and supports modern two-factor authentication methods, including webauthn/passkeys.

Measures adopted by the sub-processors to provide assistance to the processor

  • The sub-processors make contact details available to the processor should it need immediate assistance in the event of an incident or data breach.
  • The sub-processors keep regular backups of data and make them available as needed.
  • The sub-processors operate highly-available infrastructure with service-level guarantees, ensuring a consistent and stable service for the processor.

Description of the specific technical and organisational measures to be taken by the processor to be able to provide assistance to the controller.

  • The processor makes contact details available to the controller through which they can request assistance. The processor makes every effort to respond as fast as possible and prioritises responding to cases concerning data breaches or privacy incidents to the extent possible. The processor also offers paid plans which offer a guaranteed response within a certain short period of time.
  • The processor provides documentation regarding its internal systems and the most important actions controllers can perform on its dashboard. The documentation is kept up-to-date and constantly expanded where possible.
  • The processor makes its source code publicly available, allowing the controller to transparently investigate it should they wish.
  • The processor keeps database backups on a 14-day rolling basis, and offers the possibility for controllers to request a restore from a backup at a specific point in time should they accidentally delete or cause the deletion of any data. This is provided on a best-effort basis and is by no means guaranteed. Requests will be considered individually, depending on the volume or complexity of the data the controller wishes to recover.
