# Data Processing Agreements

Under the EU GDPR and related legislation in the UK and Switzerland, it is mandatory for data controllers and data processors to sign a legally binding contract, detailing the terms under which data is handled between the two entities.
Any controller using Palform as a processor (i.e. not a natural person using Palform for GDPR-exempt use only) must sign a data processing agreement before accepting form responses.

Palform makes it easy to create this agreement automatically, with just a few clicks from your dashboard.
This feature is available on all plans, including the Free plan.

## Creating an agreement

1. In your dashboard, click **Organisation** > **Settings**
2. Under **Legal agreements**, click **Create new agreement**
3. Select **Data Processing Agreement** as the agreement type, and click **Continue**
4. Complete the details, which will be automatically inserted into the generated PDF agreement
5. Click **Generate**. Your PDF agreement will be available to view and download.

:::note

The agreement will be generated already signed on behalf of Palform.
You need to sign it on behalf of your organisation for it to be a valid, legally binding contract.
Please do so at the indicated position within the document.
You do not need to send the signed copy to Palform.

:::

## Viewing created agreements

Any agreements you create can be viewed under **Organisation** > **Settings**, and their corresponding PDFs can be downloaded at any time.

## Deleting agreements

1. In your dashboard, click **Organisation** > **Settings**
2. Find the agreement you want to delete from the list
3. Click **Delete** and follow the instructions
